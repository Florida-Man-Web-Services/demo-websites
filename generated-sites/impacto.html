Mission

Language is one of the largest barriers.

A significant number of Hispanic immigrants in Gainesville are not proficient in English, which limits their freedom, agency, and mobility in the United States. IMPACTO recruits and trains Spanish-speaking UF students to teach basic English to that community.

Getting a job, asking for services, traveling, shopping, finding housing, and researching resources all require English. Basic English opens a world of access. That is the impact.

Why we exist

First- and second-generation students, teaching a community they are connected to.

IMPACTO is made of UF students whose parents are from Latin America and whose extended family still lives there. They know the privilege of U.S. citizenship — and the work it takes to earn the same opportunities.

Courses

Two classrooms. Communicative method. No charge.

Starting September 8, 2026, IMPACTO offers two free English courses to anyone from the Hispanic community living in Alachua County or the Gainesville area. Each classroom has two bilingual UF student teachers, trained by a professional foreign-language instructor (Mr. Andrés García).

01

English · Beginner

For learners who are just starting. Speaking comes first. Seats fill; a confirmation email follows registration.

02

English · Advanced beginner

For learners who have the basics and want more fluency. Review the syllabus before you register.

Fall registration: July 6 – August 21. Orientation: September 4 (parking, classrooms, attendance, instructor introductions). Students who attend at least 75% of classes receive a Certificate of Participation. Unexcused absences carry penalties; see the attendance policy on the syllabus.

Become an instructor

Watch both Zoom trainings from Mr. Andrés García in Google Classroom, then request a 10-minute interview with the president or course coordinator. Interviews are not meant to reject people — they confirm you can teach the content in the proper format. Then stay current in GroupMe.

Membership information

Besides teaching 2–3 times a month, members attend general body meetings, socials, and bimonthly tabling. An absence form exists. Instagram @uf_impacto posts events. Forms live in Google Classroom.
