Mission Statement

A significant number of Hispanic immigrants in Gainesville are not proficient in English, which limits their freedom, agency, and mobility within the United States. To promote integration, Spanish-speaking students can use their language skills to reach their community and make an impact.

Impacto, founded in the Spring of 2024, seeks to recruit and train Spanish-speaking UF students to teach basic English to the non-English-speaking Hispanic community in Gainesville. As the University of Florida has a substantial Hispanic-American student population, we can help bridge the gap in language-learning in Gainesville and Alachua and break barriers that impede the Hispanic immigrant community's agency.

Language is one of the largest barriers. Getting a job, asking for services, traveling, shopping, renting housing, and researching resources require English. Simply knowing basic English opens up an entire world of accessibility.

Course Registration

Starting September 8th of 2026, IMPACTO will offer two variations of English courses, free of charge, to anyone from the Hispanic community who also resides in Alachua County or the Gainesville Area. A communicative method will be used that will prioritize speaking. Each classroom will have two Spanish-speaking bilingual UF students trained by a professional foreign language teacher (Mr. Andrés García).
