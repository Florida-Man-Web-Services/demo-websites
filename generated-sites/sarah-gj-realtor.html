Landmarks, parks & culture

City center

Downtown Gainesville

The walkable heart of the city, from the historic courthouse square to brick-lined streets of shops and restaurants.

Historic theatre

Hippodrome State Theatre

Gainesville's professional theatre, housed in a former federal building in the downtown core.

Historic landmark

The Thomas Center

A Mediterranean Revival landmark and Gainesville cultural center.

Botanical gardens

Kanapaha Botanical Gardens

A 68-acre collection of distinctive gardens in southwest Gainesville.

Geological state park

Devil’s Millhopper

A National Natural Landmark centered on a 120-foot-deep limestone sinkhole.

State preserve

Paynes Prairie

An expansive preserve and defining natural landscape south of Gainesville.

Wetlands park

Sweetwater Wetlands Park

A 125-acre park of trails and boardwalks where filtered water flows toward Paynes Prairie, rich with birds and wildlife.
