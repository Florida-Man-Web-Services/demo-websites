# Call the public Gainesville AI 411 directory

Look up Gainesville businesses and events on the public AI 411 landing or voice line. AI 411 is a local directory and events operator. It is not a sales pitch and not a hosted receptionist for one business.

## Before you start

You need:

- A phone, to talk to the operator
- Optional: a browser, to open the landing

This line is not emergency service. For medical, police, or fire help, hang up and call 911.

## Call the directory

1. Open the [Gainesville AI 411 landing](https://ai411.floridamanweb.online/ai411/).
   `https://ai411.floridamanweb.online/` redirects to `/ai411/`. The same page is also at [https://floridamanweb.online/ai411/](https://floridamanweb.online/ai411/).
2. Read **Looking for a Gainesville business or event?** Call AI 411 and ask for the local directory.
   The landing does not print the voice number. If you already have the public line, dial it.
3. Listen for the greeting: "AI 411 in Gainesville. What do you need?"
   The operator identifies as A411 / Gainesville AI 411. It is an AI on a live call.
4. State one request: a Gainesville business, an event, or a community notice.
5. Optional: If the operator is silent after the greeting, say whether you want events, food, or a phone number.
6. Optional: Ask the operator to text you links if you want URLs.

When the operator has a listed phone number, it speaks the number. It does not place that outbound call for you, book tickets, or hold reservations.

If directory data is unavailable, the operator says it cannot access that information and does not guess. You can try another angle or call back later.

Voice for this product is served at [voice.flmanbiosci.net](https://voice.flmanbiosci.net). You do not need that host to talk to the directory.

## What you can ask

- A Gainesville business by name, including hours or a listed phone number
- Local events, including tonight, tomorrow, this weekend, movies, or the Hippodrome
- A community event or notice you want to post
- What this line is: Gainesville AI 411, Florida Man Web Services' information line

Do not use this line for medical, legal, or financial advice.

## Handle an emergency

If there is immediate danger, hang up and call **911**. Do not stay on AI 411 for medical, police, or fire emergencies.

## Optional personal page

The landing includes a separate **Prefer a personal page?** form. You can also ask on the call (for example, "make me a page" or "take my page down"). A personal page is optional, built from interests you have shared, and is not a business website.

## Request a business demo instead

If you want a free demo website callback, use **Free demo website callback** on the same landing. That path is a short AI call about your business. It is not the directory operator.
