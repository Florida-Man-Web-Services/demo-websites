# Category design directions

Paste the matching block into `{{CATEGORY_DIRECTION}}` when filling the standard prompt.  
Directions set **mood + section emphasis**, not a fixed template — each business must still get a unique palette, type system, and motif.

If a business’s `search_category` is missing, map from `category_label` or nearest sibling.

---

## accountants

**Mood:** Clinical precision, quiet trust, paper-and-ink calm. Light or deep navy/ink surfaces; restrained gold or forest accent optional.  
**Type:** Clean grotesque + tabular numbers; optional subtle serif for headlines.  
**Emphasize:** Services (tax, bookkeeping, advisory as *general* offerings), discretion, clear contact CTA.  
**Avoid:** Startup purple, emoji, fake “$2M saved for clients.”

## auto repair

**Mood:** Garage competence — charcoal, safety orange or signal yellow, steel gray.  
**Type:** Condensed industrial headlines + readable sans body.  
**Emphasize:** Diagnostics, maintenance, A/C/tires if category fits, honest process, call-to-shop.  
**Motif:** Grid, wrench geometry, hazard stripe (subtle).

## bakeries

**Mood:** Warm flour-dusted mornings — cream, cocoa, soft terracotta.  
**Type:** Soft serif or rounded display + friendly sans.  
**Emphasize:** Fresh goods, categories of bake, visit window / address.  
**Avoid:** Fake “baked this morning at 4am” schedules unless known.

## barbershops

**Mood:** Classic shop craft — oxblood, cream, brass, navy, pinstripe.  
**Type:** Strong display (slab or condensed) + readable body.  
**Emphasize:** Cuts, fades, beard work, chair CTA, local pride.  
**Motif:** Pole rhythm, stripe, scissors geometry — not cartoon.

## bars

**Mood:** Night energy — near-black, neon accent (one color), low amber light.  
**Type:** Tight tracking display + clean UI sans.  
**Emphasize:** Atmosphere, what’s pouring (general), location near campus/downtown if address fits, visit CTA.  
**Avoid:** Invented cocktail menus with prices; fake celebrity DJ nights.

## cafes

**Mood:** Slow morning light — warm neutrals, single botanical or ceramic accent.  
**Type:** Humanist sans or soft serif headlines.  
**Emphasize:** Coffee/tea craft, place to work or meet, address.  
**Motif:** Steam, cup silhouette, paper grain.

## car wash

**Mood:** Clean water and shine — cool blues, white foam, glossy black.  
**Type:** Bold geometric sans.  
**Emphasize:** Wash types, detailing if category fits, easy in-and-out, location.  
**Motif:** Droplets, arcs, reflective panels (CSS).

## cleaning services

**Mood:** Fresh, ordered, reliable — white/soft sage/sky, crisp lines.  
**Type:** Friendly professional sans.  
**Emphasize:** Residential/commercial general offerings, simple process, call to schedule.  
**Avoid:** Fake “1000 homes cleaned.”

## clothing boutiques

**Mood:** Editorial retail — generous whitespace or moody boutique dark; one fashion accent.  
**Type:** High-fashion tracking or elegant serif.  
**Emphasize:** Style POV, categories of goods, visit.  
**Avoid:** Fake lookbooks with stolen brand names.

## contractors

**Mood:** Built, measured, solid — blueprint blue, concrete gray, timber accent.  
**Type:** Technical sans + mono for measurements aesthetic.  
**Emphasize:** Build/remodel general services, trust, contact for estimate.  
**Motif:** Grid, level line, stamp.

## convenience stores

**Mood:** Quick neighborhood stop — bright practical, clear hierarchy, easy scan.  
**Type:** Bold utilitarian.  
**Emphasize:** Hours language if known, location, everyday essentials (general).  
**Avoid:** Fake inventory lists.

## dance studios

**Mood:** Motion and form — dark stage or light studio; one vivid accent.  
**Type:** Expressive display + clear body.  
**Emphasize:** Styles/classes as general offerings, community, contact.  
**Motif:** Arc, rhythm lines — not stock dancers.

## daycares

**Mood:** Safe, warm, playful-but-calm — soft pastels with strong contrast for text.  
**Type:** Rounded friendly sans (not childish chaos).  
**Emphasize:** Care philosophy in general terms, contact for enrollment info.  
**Avoid:** Invented curricula, license numbers, staff bios.

## dry cleaners

**Mood:** Crisp garment care — white shirt, navy, gentle steam blue.  
**Type:** Clean classic sans.  
**Emphasize:** Cleaning/pressing/alterations if plausible, turnaround language only if known, location.

## electricians

**Mood:** Voltage and safety — deep charcoal, electric yellow or cyan bolt accent.  
**Type:** Strong geometric sans.  
**Emphasize:** Residential/commercial electrical general services, emergency call if appropriate without false “24/7” unless known.  
**Motif:** Bolt, circuit, glow edge.

## food trucks

**Mood:** Street energy — bold color blocking, high contrast, mobile.  
**Type:** Chunk display + simple body.  
**Emphasize:** Cuisine vibe, find-us (address/Maps), call if listed.  
**Avoid:** Full invented menus with prices.

## grocery stores

**Mood:** Abundant practical — fresh greens, produce accents, clear aisle hierarchy.  
**Type:** Friendly retail sans.  
**Emphasize:** Neighborhood groceries, specialties only if known (international, seafood, etc. from name), location.

## gyms

**Mood:** Discipline and energy — black/graphite, one hot accent (orange/red).  
**Type:** Heavy condensed headlines.  
**Emphasize:** Training focus from name/category (e.g. yoga vs gym), visit/contact.  
**Avoid:** Fake transformation photos/metrics.

## hair salons

**Mood:** Polished beauty — soft glam light or chic dark; rose gold / deep plum accents carefully.  
**Type:** Elegant script sparingly OR refined sans/serif pair (script only for wordmark accents).  
**Emphasize:** Cuts, color, styling as general services, book-by-phone CTA.

## handyman

**Mood:** Capable neighbor — warm wood, tool-metal gray, trustworthy blue.  
**Type:** Workwear sans.  
**Emphasize:** Odd jobs / repairs / installs as general list, call for help, local.  
**Motif:** Tape measure ticks, hardware silhouettes (minimal).

## hvac

**Mood:** Climate comfort — cool blue + warm amber duality, clean panels.  
**Type:** Technical professional sans.  
**Emphasize:** Cooling/heating service & repair (general), Florida heat context without cliché overload, call.

## insurance agents

**Mood:** Steady counsel — navy, cream, restrained green.  
**Type:** Conservative serif + sans.  
**Emphasize:** Guidance/contact for quotes; no fake policy claims.

## jewelers

**Mood:** Precious focus — black velvet or soft ivory, single metal accent (gold/silver).  
**Type:** Refined serif display.  
**Emphasize:** Craft, repair/custom if category-true, visit.  
**Motif:** Facet geometry, thin rules.

## landscapers

**Mood:** Outdoor structure — leaf green, soil brown, sky.  
**Type:** Natural humanist.  
**Emphasize:** Lawn/landscape services general, curb appeal, contact.  
**Motif:** Leaf vein, horizon line.

## laundromats

**Mood:** Clean cycles — soft blue-gray, white, gentle motion.  
**Type:** Simple friendly sans.  
**Emphasize:** Machines/self-serve (general), location, hours language if known.  
**Motif:** Bubble, drum circle (abstract).

## lawn care

**Mood:** Fresh cut — grass green, sun-washed neutrals.  
**Type:** Clear outdoor sans.  
**Emphasize:** Mowing/maintenance general, reliability, call.  
**Avoid:** Fake chemical certifications.

## martial arts

**Mood:** Discipline and respect — black belt contrast, mat gray, one belt-color accent.  
**Type:** Strong display, calm body.  
**Emphasize:** Art/style if in name, training community, contact.  
**Motif:** Enso-like circle or clean diagonal — culturally respectful, not costume.

## movers

**Mood:** Motion and care — truck-strong blues/oranges, clear steps.  
**Type:** Bold utilitarian.  
**Emphasize:** Local moving help (general), process steps, call for quote.  
**Avoid:** Fake fleet size.

## nail salons

**Mood:** Glam clean — soft blush, ink, glossy accents.  
**Type:** Modern beauty sans.  
**Emphasize:** Mani/pedi/gel as general services, visit/call.

## notary

**Mood:** Official calm — seal navy, parchment cream.  
**Type:** Traditional serif + clear sans.  
**Emphasize:** Mobile or office notary availability from name, appointment via phone.  
**Motif:** Seal/stamp abstract.

## painters

**Mood:** Color craft — confident swatches, clean white ground or studio gray.  
**Type:** Contemporary sans.  
**Emphasize:** Interior/exterior painting general, prep quality language (honest), estimate CTA.  
**Motif:** Brush stroke geometry, chip samples (CSS).

## pest control

**Mood:** Protective clarity — deep green or slate, alert amber carefully.  
**Type:** Straight-talking sans.  
**Emphasize:** Inspection/treatment general, home protection, call.  
**Avoid:** Gross-out imagery; fake EPA claims.

## plumbers

**Mood:** Solid trade — navy, copper, clean white.  
**Type:** Work-strong sans.  
**Emphasize:** Repair/install general, call for service.  
**Motif:** Pipe elbow geometry, drop.

## print shops

**Mood:** Ink and register — paper white, pure black, CMYK accent sparingly.  
**Type:** Mix of clean grotesque + mono labels.  
**Emphasize:** Print/sign/branding general services from category, contact.  
**Motif:** Crop marks, registration.

## real estate agents

**Mood:** Open house light — airy neutrals, one confident accent.  
**Type:** Editorial sans/serif.  
**Emphasize:** Local market help (general), contact.  
**Avoid:** Fake listings.

## restaurants

**Mood:** Cuisine-led — let name/cuisine drive palette (e.g. Persian spice warmth, not generic red-check).  
**Type:** Appetite-friendly display.  
**Emphasize:** Cuisine story, visit, general dish categories not full fake menu prices.  
**Social proof:** rating if provided.

## roofers

**Mood:** Shelter and height — slate, storm blue, metal silver.  
**Type:** Strong industrial.  
**Emphasize:** Roofing/gutters/insulation if in name, inspection CTA, local.  
**Avoid:** Fake storm-chaser urgency.

## spas

**Mood:** Quiet restoration — stone, sand, soft sage, low contrast chrome.  
**Type:** Light elegant sans or soft serif.  
**Emphasize:** Wellness services general, appointment by phone.  
**Motion:** Very slow/none.

## tattoo shops

**Mood:** Ink studio — black, flash-paper cream, one saturated ink color.  
**Type:** Custom display energy + readable body.  
**Emphasize:** Custom work, styles as general, consult/call.  
**Avoid:** Fake artist portfolios or copied tattoo photos.

## tire shops

**Mood:** Grip and road — asphalt black, rubber gray, safety orange.  
**Type:** Bold automotive sans.  
**Emphasize:** Tires/service general, location, call.  
**Motif:** Tread pattern abstract.

## upholstery

**Mood:** Textile craft — linen, walnut, stitch contrast.  
**Type:** Craft serif or soft work sans.  
**Emphasize:** Repair/recover/fabric (general), bring-in or visit, call.  
**Motif:** Stitch dashes, welt line.

## veterinarians

**Mood:** Calm care — soft blue/green clinical warmth (not neon clinic).  
**Type:** Friendly professional.  
**Emphasize:** Pet care general, contact; mobile if in name.  
**Avoid:** Medical guarantees, fake clinic hours, invented doctor names.

## insurance agents / accountants / notary (shared note)

Keep claims conservative. Prefer “talk to us about…” over “we guarantee…”

**Emphasize:** Clear who/what/where/how to contact; 5+ sections; unique motif.
